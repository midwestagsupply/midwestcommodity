How we buy

We buy corn and soybeans. Three ways to do it.

Cash or spot price

Haul it in and take the posted price. Get paid on delivery, or put payment off to a date you pick.

Forward contract

Lock in a price now for grain you deliver later. Minimum 5,000 bushels, in 5,000 bushel lots.

Storage

Store it here for 5 cents a bushel a month, up to 12 months unless other arrangements have been made. Drying is charged by the schedule below. Sell when you decide the price is right.

Corn drying and shrink

Wet corn is dried here. The charge comes off your settlement, by moisture at delivery.

Shrink: 1.4% per point of moisture above 14%.

There are two schedules. Above 16 percent moisture they are exactly the same, running from 15 cents a bushel up to 56 cents at 35 percent. They only differ underneath that: corn we buy is 5 cents from 15.001 to 15.5 and 10 cents from 15.501 to 16.0, while corn already in storage is a flat 10 cents anywhere below 16.
